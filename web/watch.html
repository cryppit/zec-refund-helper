Waiting on a refund? Watch it here.

A deposit stuck in the Omni bridge moves in steps: the refund is requested, its lock runs out, someone executes it, the coin goes home. Paste the deposits you are waiting on. The page checks each one, re-checks every 10 minutes while it stays open, and tells you the moment one changes and what comes next.

Anything after the hash on a line is your note: a ticket number or a name. Notes stay in this browser.

For wallet and exchange support teams

Every stuck-deposit ticket, in one place

When a customer's ZEC or BTC never arrives through the Omni bridge, the fix is the bridge's own refund, and it takes days: a request, a lock, an execute. This page keeps watch so nobody has to re-check by hand.

  • 1. Paste every open caseOne deposit hash per line, with the ticket number after it. The list is kept in this browser only, so it never reaches us or anyone else.
  • 2. Leave the tab openEach deposit is re-checked every 10 minutes. A row that changed is marked, the tab title counts the changes, and the browser can notify you.
  • 3. Copy a summary into the ticketOne button writes each deposit's state, why, and what changed, in plain words you can paste to the customer.
  • 4. Tell the customer the exact next stepOpen any row for the full check: the steps, what each costs, and the only address the coin can go back to.
  • Flag the ones that can't come backAbsorbed below the minimum, a placeholder refund address, too old to request, or behind the bridge's years-long lock: the page says so, so nobody promises a refund that can't happen.

For the person waiting

Paste your own deposit and leave the page open, or come back later: it remembers the list in this browser and shows what changed since you last looked.

Nothing leaves your browser

The list and the last states are saved in this browser only. Each check reads the same public sources as a single check (the chain's explorer, the Omni bridge indexer, NEAR), which see your IP address, as any website does.

No accountNo serverNo keys

It never moves coins

Watching is read-only. Requesting or executing a refund is a NEAR call you sign yourself, and the coin can only go back to the refund address the deposit names. Nobody needs a seed phrase or a key.
