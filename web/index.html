How a check works

From your transaction hash to the way back, read from public data only.

  1. 1. Pastethe deposit's transaction hash
  2. 2. Readthe chain, the bridge indexer and the NEAR contract
  3. 3. Classifycredited, stuck, requested, ready, executed, refunded or not recoverable
  4. 4. Stepsthe exact calls left, what each costs, signed by you
  5. 5. Back homeonly to the refund address the bridge allows

What it tells apart

Every deposit gets one of these, from the chain and the bridge contract.

  • CreditedThe bridge took it in; nothing to do.
  • Stuck, refundableNever credited; the refund path is open.
  • Requested or readyWaiting out the lock, or anyone can execute.
  • RefundedBack at the refund address.
  • Not recoverableBelow the minimum, a placeholder address, too old, or the 100-year lock.
