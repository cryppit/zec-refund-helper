Sending ZEC? Check the address first.

Zcash has several kinds of address, and not every wallet can pay every kind. Paste one to see whether a transparent-only or a shielded wallet can send to it, what to do next, and which refund the Omni bridge could make to it. It also catches typos before they cost you.

Every kind of Zcash address

The prefix tells you the kind; the checksum tells you whether it was copied right. This is what the check answers for each.

Testnet addresses (tm, t2, textest1, utest1, ztestsapling1) are recognised and flagged: real ZEC sent to them is lost. Newer Revision 2 unified addresses are recognised but not yet read, so the check tells you to let your wallet decide.

On the Omni bridge

Which refund an address can take

A ZEC deposit the bridge never credits can be refunded to the refund address its sender named. The Zcash connector pays only two kinds of receiver, so the address decides which refund is possible.

Check a stuck deposit →
  • Shielded refund

    To an Orchard receiver. bridge-cli builds it, and it is the default. Since NU6.3 it lands in the Ironwood pool, where your wallet reads it like any shielded note.

  • Transparent refund

    To a t-address, a TEX address or a unified address's transparent receiver. near-cli can send the call.

  • Neither

    A Sapling address, or a unified address with only a Sapling receiver. A refund to it could never be executed, so don't name it as a refund address.

What it catches before you send

  • A typoEvery Zcash address carries a checksum. One wrong or missing character fails it, and the check says don't send.
  • Shielded money to a TEX addressA TEX address must be paid from transparent funds. A shielded wallet has to move the ZEC to a transparent address first.
  • A shielded address for a transparent walletA transparent-only wallet can't pay a Sapling address or a shielded-only unified address. Ask for a t-address.
  • A testnet or Sprout addressTestnet addresses take no real ZEC, and Sprout has accepted no new funds since 2020.
  • Not a Zcash address at allA Bitcoin or Ethereum address pasted by mistake is rejected, not guessed at.

Decoded from scratch

Unified addresses are decoded per ZIP 316, including the F4Jumble step, and tested on the official test vectors. TEX addresses follow ZIP 320.

Offline and private

The address is read in your browser. Nothing is looked up or sent anywhere, so the check works with no connection and nobody learns which address you checked.
